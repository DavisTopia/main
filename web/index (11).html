01What makes Nashville Tax Solutions different?

Nashville Tax Solutions is built around honest guidance, proactive communication, and a more disciplined approach to serious tax matters. Our goal is not to overwhelm you with vague promises. It is to help you understand where you stand, what may apply, and what the right next step could be.

02Do you work with clients nationwide?

Yes. We work with individuals and businesses in all 50 states. While our offices are in Florida and Tennessee, our team can help taxpayers nationwide.

03What types of tax issues do you handle?

Our services include tax preparation, back tax support, IRS audits, tax liens, payment plans, Offers in Compromise, Innocent Spouse Relief, passport revocation matters, hardship tax relief, payroll taxes, bookkeeping, and business advisory services.

04What is the first step if I need help?

Call us at 855-627-4829 or use the contact form to request a conversation. We’ll discuss your concern, learn what you’re looking for, and explain potential next steps. Any recommendation depends on reviewing your particular circumstances.

05What if I have fallen far behind?

Reach out as soon as you can and share what you know about the missing returns, balances, or notices. We can discuss your situation and what information is needed to assess possible ways to get current and address collection concerns.

06Do I need to have everything organized before I reach out?

No. You can start with the information you have. If you have IRS or state notices, prior returns, or balance details, keep them nearby; our team can help identify what documents may be useful after learning about your situation.

07Will this be a sales call or a real strategy conversation?

An initial call is a chance to outline your concern and determine what information is needed. Developing an informed strategy requires a more detailed review of the facts and records; we’ll explain the appropriate next step and any applicable engagement terms before work begins.

08Do you provide support after the immediate issue is resolved?

Yes. We can provide guidance on staying compliant and discuss ongoing tax-related services that may fit your needs after the immediate matter is addressed.

09Is my information confidential?

We treat client information as confidential and use it to provide and manage requested services. Please avoid sending sensitive tax documents through ordinary email before speaking with our team about a secure way to share them.

10What kind of results have you achieved for clients?

Our website reports that Nashville Tax Solutions has resolved more than 2,788 IRS cases, helped over 3,984 individuals and businesses become tax-compliant, and saved clients more than $127.9 million in tax penalties. Outcomes vary; prior results do not guarantee a particular result in a future matter.
