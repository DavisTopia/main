Founder

Christopher Bennett, Sr.

Founder & CEO, Enrolled Agent

At Nashville Tax Solutions, our story goes beyond numbers. It is rooted in real relationships and meaningful outcomes. Founded by Christopher Bennett, Sr., an experienced Enrolled Agent and industry leader, the firm is built on the belief that tax resolution should never feel overwhelming or impersonal.

Born in Sussex, NJ, and later moving to Sebastian, FL, Christopher's journey is a blend of academic achievement and athletic discipline. A high school standout in soccer, football, and track, he carried that dedication to Saint Leo University in Florida, where he majored in Sport Management and studied advanced accounting.

After graduation, his career in the tax industry began in marketing and sales management and evolved through several roles across the tax resolution sector. His defining milestone came in 2015, when he became an Enrolled Agent, authorized to represent taxpayers before the IRS and state governments.

His vision has shaped a client-first culture grounded in ethical practice, creative problem solving, and a commitment to delivering real results.
