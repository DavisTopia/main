Common types of Penalty Abatements

1. Reasonable Cause Abatement: This type of abatement is granted when taxpayers can demonstrate that their failure to comply with tax obligations was due to circumstances beyond their control.

2. First-Time Penalty Abatement: FTA is available for taxpayers who have a clean compliance history and have not incurred any significant penalties in the previous three years.

3. Statutory Exception Abatement: Some penalties can be abated if taxpayers can prove that they fall under a specific statutory exception.
